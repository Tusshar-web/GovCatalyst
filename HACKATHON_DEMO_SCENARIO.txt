📋 SCENARIO PREMISE & CHALLENGE DETAIL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) needs to transition from manual,
high-risk, slow, and expensive concrete bridge structural inspections to an 
automated UAV/Drone & Computer Vision inspection process. 

Under India's General Financial Rules (GFR) Rule 194, NHAI will define outcome-based
targets, select a DPIIT-recognized startup, run an outcome-based sandbox pilot, 
and transition it to a direct scale-up contract on GeM without traditional 
turnover and experience requirements.
